How it works

Your ward is running in two minutes.

  1. Sign in

    Use your Google account, or Sign in with Apple. No passwords to make up, forget or share.

  2. Create or join a ward

    A new ward gets a five-digit code like 47291. Colleagues join with it — nobody else can see inside.

  3. Post, reply, acknowledge

    Write a note on a patient. Your team sees it instantly, replies in the thread, and taps Acknowledge when it’s done.

Privacy

Clinical notes deserve better than a group chat.

FolioMed has no ads, no tracking and nothing to sell. It exists to move information between the people caring for a patient — and no further.

Ward-private

Row-level security in the database means a ward’s notes can’t be read from outside it — even by a bug in the app.

No passwords

Sign-in goes through Google or Apple, so there’s no FolioMed password to leak, reuse or reset.

Yours to delete

Delete your account from Profile at any time. Delete a ward and everything in it is erased permanently.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every feature, for every ward, with no trial period and no card required.

Where are the iPhone and Android apps?

They’re on their way to the App Store and Google Play. Until then, FolioMed runs in any modern browser at foliomed.app/app — on a phone it works like an app.

Who can see our patients and notes?

Only members of your ward — people who joined with its five-digit code. Access is enforced by the database, not just hidden in the app.

Why only Google and Apple sign-in?

So there are no passwords to manage. Your Google or Apple account already has strong protection such as two-factor authentication.

Does FolioMed replace our hospital records system?

No. FolioMed is for the conversation around a patient — handovers, orders, follow-ups. Your hospital’s record system stays the source of truth.
